\chapter{User Roles and Use Cases}

\section{User Roles}

The application distinguishes between two main user roles:

\begin{description}
  \item[Manager] Creates, edits, and deletes jobs. Assigns jobs to workers. Tracks the team's location on the map in real time. Has access to an overview of all jobs and their statuses. Can communicate with workers via chat.

  \item[Worker] Can only see jobs assigned to them. Updates the status of a job (e.g.\ \textit{In Progress}, \textit{Done}). Uploads photo documentation to a job. Shares their GPS location with the manager. Can communicate with the manager via chat.
\end{description}

\section{Use Cases}

\subsection{Manager}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.06\textwidth}p{0.3\textwidth}p{0.54\textwidth}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{\#} & \textbf{Use case} & \textbf{Description} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  UC-M1 & Login           & Manager logs in via Google SSO or email and password. \\
  UC-M2 & Create job      & Manager creates a new job with a title, description, GPS coordinates, and deadline. \\
  UC-M3 & Assign job      & Manager assigns a job to one or more workers. \\
  UC-M4 & Edit / delete job & Manager edits or deletes an existing job. \\
  UC-M5 & Job overview    & Manager sees a list of all jobs with their current statuses. \\
  UC-M6 & Team map        & Manager sees live worker locations and job site pins on the map. \\
  UC-M7 & Chat            & Manager communicates with workers via real-time chat. \\
  UC-M8 & Push notification & Manager receives a notification when a worker changes a job status to \textit{Done}. \\
  \bottomrule
  \caption{Use cases --- Manager}
\end{longtable}
