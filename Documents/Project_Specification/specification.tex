\chapter{Application Overview}
\section{Purpose of the Application}

WorkFlow is a mobile application designed for coordinating field teams. It allows managers to create and assign work orders (jobs) to field workers, track their location in real time on a map, and communicate with them through a built-in chat. Workers receive push notifications about new assignments, can update job statuses, and upload photo documentation directly from the field.

The goal of the application is to replace inefficient email or phone-based coordination and give management an instant overview of job progress and team location.

\chapter*{User Roles}

The application defines two distinct user roles: \textbf{Manager} and \textbf{Worker}. Each role has a dedicated interface and a specific set of permissions. Roles are strictly separate — a user registered as a Manager cannot act as a Worker and vice versa.

\subsection*{Role Overview}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\bfseries}l X}
\toprule
Role & Description \\
\midrule
Manager & A team leader responsible for creating and managing projects, assigning jobs to workers, and monitoring worker activity. \\
Worker & A field employee who receives job assignments, updates task progress, uploads professional certificates, and communicates with their manager. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsection*{Role Assignment}

Roles are assigned at the point of registration and cannot be changed afterwards. A Manager registers directly through the application. A Worker can only join the system by receiving an invite link generated by a Manager — upon signing up via this link, the Worker is automatically assigned the Worker role and connected to the Manager who sent the invitation.
